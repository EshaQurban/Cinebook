About the movie

The Jailer movie, starring Rajinikanth, revolves around the complexities of the prison system, exploring themes of justice, morality, and redemption. As a jailer, Rajinikanth's character navigates the challenges of maintaining order while confronting corruption and social inequality within the system. The film delves into moral dilemmas, character development, and intense action, making it a thought-provoking and engaging watch.

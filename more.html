About the movie

Bella tells the story of an unexpected day in the lives of two people in New York City—José, a former soccer star with a troubled past, and Nina, a young waitress who finds herself pregnant and unsure about her future. When Nina is fired from her job, José walks out with her, and what follows is a transformative day that deeply affects both of them. As the story unfolds, José reveals the tragedy that ended his promising sports career, while Nina grapples with the decision about her unborn child. Through their conversations and the emotional support José offers, Bella explores themes of forgiveness, redemption, the value of life, and the unexpected beauty that can emerge from pain.
